How a loading system comes together

A clear route from first question to working system.

Material flow

  1. Truck receiving feederTakes material from trucks where this suits the operation.
  2. Screening, where requiredUsed only when the material and process need it.
  3. Transfer conveyorMoves material along the project-defined route.
  4. Fines or additional conveyor, where requiredAdded depending on flow and layout.
  5. Ship loading conveyorCarries material to the ship-side loading position.
  6. Telescopic spout or discharge arrangementChosen to suit the material and vessel.
  7. Discharge into the vesselThe final arrangement is project-specific.

How a project works
