Bulk Material Ship Loader

Bulk cargo handling shaped by material flow, transfers, vessel access and berth conditions.

Overview

Bulk loading depends on how material flows, how it is transferred and how the ship can be reached. We shape each bulk material ship loader around those facts, including dust-management needs where the site requires them.

Typical configuration

Receiving point, transfer conveyors and a ship loading conveyor ending at a spout or discharge arrangement, adapted to the material and berth.

Where it fits

  • Coal, iron ore, fertiliser, grain and mineral terminals
  • Mixed-cargo berths handling several bulk materials
  • Sites planning a new or upgraded loading route

Key features

Designed around material properties and flow
Transfer arrangements suited to bulk cargo
Configurable discharge toward the vessel
Dust-management needs considered per project
Scope matched to berth and vessel access
